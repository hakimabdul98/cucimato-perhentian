Cucimato began with RM50 and a stranger's kindness. In 2018 I went to the island with just RM50. A man named Ameer looked after me, fed me every meal and never let me pay. I went home with that RM50 untouched, and a promise to pass his kindness on. Today Ameer is my co-founder. A trip to Phuket later showed us that great service doesn't need a high price. So we live by one motto:

“Don't judge our price until you've experienced our service.”
