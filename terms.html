Service Delivery Policy

What you get, when you get it, and what we need from you.

1. Our services

  • Ferry tickets between Kuala Besut Jetty and the Perhentian Islands (return or one-way)
  • Airport transfers between the airport and Kuala Besut Jetty
  • Hotel + packages and ground packages, including snorkeling trips and activities

2. How you receive your booking

  • Ferry tickets and airport transfers paid online: your receipt, invoice and e-ticket are emailed right after payment, usually within a few minutes.
  • Packages: your invoice is sent by email and WhatsApp within 24 hours. Your receipt follows after each payment.
  • Hotel requests: we check room availability and reply within 24 hours. No payment is taken until the room is confirmed.
  • The service itself is delivered on the date and time you booked.

3. On the day

  • Ferry: show your e-ticket (on your phone is fine) at Kuala Besut Jetty. For the boat back, be at your island pickup point on time.
  • Airport transfer: our driver contacts you on WhatsApp before pickup. Please keep your phone on, and tell us if your flight changes. Shared transfers leave at fixed times and need at least 2 passengers.
  • If anything changes, such as weather or delays, we tell you on WhatsApp as early as we can and offer the next best option.

4. What we need from you

  • Correct booking details and a phone number we can reach on WhatsApp.
  • Being at the right place on time. A no-show, or not being reachable, may mean losing the service without a refund.
