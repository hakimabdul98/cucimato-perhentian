Booking Terms & Refund Policy

Cucimato Perhentian Island · Kuala Besut Jetty, 22300 Besut, Terengganu, Malaysia

By booking or paying on this website, you agree to these terms. If anything is unclear, please WhatsApp us before you book.

1. Ferry tickets and airport transfers (paid online)

  • Full payment: the full amount is paid online when you book. Your booking is confirmed once the payment is successful.
  • Confirmation: your receipt, invoice and e-ticket are sent to the email you gave us. Please show the e-ticket (on your phone is fine) at the jetty or to your driver.
  • Boat and pickup times are the times you chose. Please arrive at the jetty at least 30 minutes early. Late arrivals may have to take a later boat, subject to seats.
  • Airport pickup: please book flights that land within the pickup times shown. If your flight is delayed, WhatsApp us as soon as you can.

2. Hotel and ground packages (invoice)

  • Deposit: a non-refundable deposit (shown on your invoice) is required to confirm and secure the booking, by the date on the invoice.
  • Balance payment: the remaining balance is payable in full by the date on the invoice.

3. Cancellation and refunds

  • Ferry tickets and airport transfers: payments are non-refundable once the booking is confirmed, whatever the reason for cancellation. You may change the date instead (see section 4).
  • Packages: the deposit is strictly non-refundable once paid, whatever the reason for cancellation.
  • If we cancel: if we cannot provide a service you paid for, we will offer a new date or a refund of the amount paid for that service.
  • Double or wrong payments: if you were charged twice or by mistake, WhatsApp or email us with your reference number and we will refund the extra amount to the original payment method, usually within 7–14 working days.

4. Changes to your booking

  • Booking amendments: please tell us about any change at least 14 days before your travel date. Changes depend on availability and may incur additional charges.
  • Change of travel date: requests to change the travel date depend on availability and a service charge may apply.

5. Weather and sea conditions

Boats only run when it is safe. We are not responsible for cancellation, postponement or disruption caused by bad or unsafe weather, or by decisions of the boat operators or the authorities. In that case we will help you move to the next available boat or date, under the policies above.

6. Payment

Online payments are processed securely by HitPay (FPX online banking, DuitNow QR, e-wallets and cards). We never see or store your card or bank login details. For other ways to pay, please contact our team.
